Our NDT Services

Comprehensive conventional non-destructive testing methods tailored to your industry requirements

Ultrasonic Testing (UT)

Conventional ultrasonic testing using high-frequency sound waves to detect internal flaws, measure wall thickness, and evaluate weld integrity in pipelines, pressure vessels, and structural components.

  • Weld inspection & flaw detection
  • Wall thickness measurement
  • Lamination detection
  • Corrosion mapping

Radiographic Testing (RT)

Conventional radiographic testing using X-ray and gamma ray sources to produce images of internal structures, revealing defects such as porosity, slag, and incomplete fusion in welds.

  • X-ray & gamma ray inspection
  • Weld radiography
  • Castings inspection
  • Film & digital radiography

Magnetic Particle Testing (MT)

Magnetic particle inspection to detect surface and near-surface discontinuities in ferromagnetic materials, ideal for welds, shafts, and structural steel components in oil and gas facilities.

  • Surface crack detection
  • Wet & dry particle methods
  • Visible & fluorescent MT
  • Yoke & prod techniques

Our NDT Process

Systematic conventional NDT workflows ensuring consistent, professional inspection deliverables

1

Job Setup & Procedure Review

Review of inspection scope, applicable codes and standards (ASME, API, AWS), and preparation of NDT procedures and technique sheets

2

Surface Preparation

Cleaning and surface conditioning of test areas to ensure optimal coupling for UT, proper magnetization for MT, and clean surfaces for PT examination

3

NDT Examination

Certified technicians perform conventional NDT methods — UT, RT, MT, PT, or VT — following approved procedures and industry standards

4

Evaluation & Reporting

Results evaluated against acceptance criteria, with detailed inspection reports including indications, interpretations, and recommendations

5

Documentation & Delivery

Comprehensive test reports delivered to clients with digital records, technique sheets, and radiographic films archived for traceability

6

Follow-up & Support

Ongoing technical support, results consultation, and recommendations for repair, re-inspection, or additional NDT methods as needed
